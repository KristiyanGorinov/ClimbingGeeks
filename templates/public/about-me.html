About This Project

ClimbingGeeks represents my first major Django project - a platform where two of my passions converge. This website is designed to be a hub for climbing enthusiasts where you can:

  • Join and create climbing clubs
  • Discover upcoming competitions
  • Connect with fellow climbers
  • Share experiences and tips
  • Track your climbing progress

As a beginner in Django, this project has been an incredible learning experience. Every feature, from user authentication to database design, has been a step forward in my web development journey.
